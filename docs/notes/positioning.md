# Positioning (internal, never published)

From the owner's own notes and earlier research sessions in Sep and Oct 2026. Re-verify any market claim before using it in sales material; nothing here has been re-checked for this package.

## One line

AI that reads the records an Indian factory already keeps, in the owner's language, and says plainly what is live.

## Why the site is built the way it is

- The buyer is a factory owner or an accounts person who lives on Tally, WhatsApp and phone. The first screen must say what ARQ does for that person, not what AI is.
- The owner considers ARQ's SME apps easy to copy and says sales is the bottleneck. A website cannot fix distribution, so it is built to do three narrow jobs: be believable in ten seconds, route to WhatsApp, and give partners a page they can send.
- Differentiation that is real and hard to copy quickly: Indian-language operation across the whole product, channel partnerships in GIDC and MIDC estates, vertical depth module by module, and honest status labelling.
- Earlier research found a closely related competitor doing a similar Tally intelligence job and that Tally's own embedded AI is a tailwind more than a threat because it fills Tally with cleaner data. The site names none of them. It shows the product working on sample data instead.
- The research insight that upstream capture beats downstream dashboards for batch-entry Tally users is not on the site as a claim. It shapes the Custom apps page (paper to record) and the hero (records you already have).

## Audiences and the single action for each

| Audience | Action the site pushes |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | WhatsApp, or start the free trial |
| Accounts person | Read the Astra page and the FAQ, then WhatsApp |
| Tally partner, CA | Partners page, then WhatsApp |
| Restaurant or hotel owner | Early list |

## What the site must never do

Overclaim, name a competitor, name a prospect, quote a price, or look like a generic AI lab.
