# ARQ Astra

Status on the site: live. 14-day free trial. Pricing on enquiry.

## What it is

A multi-tenant SaaS that connects TallyPrime to an AI-assisted dashboard and copilot, with English, Hindi, Marathi and Gujarati support. A Windows connector app reads TallyPrime through its XML gateway in read-only mode and syncs to ARQ's cloud. Brand name in older notes: ArcAstra, ERQ Extra (typos and variants; use ARQ Astra).

## Who buys

Owners and accounts staff of Tally-using manufacturers, mainly in GIDC and MIDC estates. Distribution is planned through Tally channel partners. A Vapi-based Tally dealership is the likely first channel partner. Do not name partners on the site.

## Confirmed live for new customers (Oct 2026)

Receivables ageing from Tally or Excel, reminder drafts (copy for WhatsApp, no auto-send), MSME 45-day alert, dead stock, owner daily brief.

## In suite description but not in the live list

Statutory compliance (MSMED payments, ROC filings, pollution board returns, labour codes), WhatsApp daily brief, Marg ERP as a second source (in progress). The site shows these under "Coming next", pending the owner's OK.

## How the site shows it

- Product page "What works today" lists only the five live items.
- Mockup: answer panel with a sample overdue-receivables question, key facts computed from `data/sample-mockups.json`, a three-row table and a reminder draft labelled as a draft.
- FAQ answers: read-only, no auto-send, four languages, Windows app, hosting by request, pricing on enquiry.

## Say

"Reads from Tally. Does not write to it." "Reminders are drafts. You press send." "Ask in your own language."

## Do not say

Data stays in India or on your premises. Numbers are calculated by code only. Secure in any strong sense. Works with Marg. Any price, ROI or customer number. Any AI vendor or model name.
