# ARQ custom apps

Status on the site: available as a service. Not a product. No pricing, no timelines.

## What it is

Small, focused apps for one process at a time, for Indian factories and MSMEs. Sold under the ARQ ONE AI Labs banner. The working method the owner has used in demos: agree one journey, build a working demo on dummy or sample data first, show it, then build for real.

## Patterns the site names

1. Paper to record. Photograph a shop-floor register or sheet, read it with software, a person confirms the unsure fields, fixed checks run on the result. This is the pattern from the material-tracking prototype. The page says "built as a prototype" and must keep saying it.
2. People and wages. Contractor or employee tracking with attendance, advances and payments, in Indian languages. In progress as a parallel project (earlier called Thekedar). The page says "in progress".
3. A dashboard over what you already keep. Excel, Tally or another ERP export turned into the few numbers checked daily.

## Process on the page

Talk, scope one journey, demo on sample data, pilot in the factory, handover. No durations promised.

## Honest note on the page

"We will tell you early if your idea is better served by a spreadsheet or an existing product." This matches the owner's habit of testing whether something is a real product before building it.

## Do not say

Fixed price, fixed timeline, client names, any industry beyond what the page says, any AI vendor, any pharma or regulated-industry project.

## Why it is on the site

It gives a visitor whose problem fits no product a reason to message, and it is the honest home for experiments that are not products yet.
