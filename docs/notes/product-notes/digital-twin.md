# ARQ Digital Twin

Status on the site: in development. Early list only. No trial. No pricing.

## What is known

The owner listed ARQ Digital Twin as a product line in the brief of 2026-10-31. No scope, design, customer or build state exists in project notes or earlier sessions. The roadmap note from Sep and Oct 2026 describes a single MSME operating system built from modules that share one data layer (Astra for money, Yantra for machines, a material module, a tax module).

## What the site says, and why

The site copy is deliberately modest and is a proposal, not a fact:

- One live picture of the plant: machines, material and money in one view.
- Planned scope, subject to change: a plant view of machines by line with latest status; money from Astra shown beside what the machines did; one place to ask questions in your language.
- "We build in order: the records first, the picture second", so Twin follows Astra and Yantra.

This framing follows the shared-data design and the constraint that hardware is off-the-shelf, white-labelled or partner-supplied, since there is no in-house hardware design. It asserts no hardware, no sensors and no timeline.
