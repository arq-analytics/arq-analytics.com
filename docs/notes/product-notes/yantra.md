# ARQ Yantra

Status on the site: live. 14-day free trial. Pricing on enquiry.

## What it is

A machinery register and maintenance tool updated by voice in Indian languages (speech to text through an Indian-language speech service). Astra covers ERP data such as sales, profit and expenses. Yantra covers machines and how production looks. Older notes call it Yantra IQ.

## Who buys

Plant owners, supervisors and maintenance teams in factories of roughly 10 to 300 workers, in engineering and fabrication, chemicals and dyes, plastics and injection moulding, textiles, pharma intermediates and food processing. The site does not list industries and does not mention pharma.

The owner wants Yantra broadened beyond supervisor-entered details to any plant-based organisation with machines. The site says "anyone in a plant with machines to look after".

## Confirmed live for new customers (Oct 2026)

Machine onboarding from Excel, nameplate photo or voice; voice breakdown and downtime log; maintenance due list; daily brief.

## How the site shows it

- "What works today": the four live items.
- Mockup: machine list with state and next service in days (overdue shown with text), and a voice log with Hindi, Marathi and Gujarati transcripts and the fields parsed from each. All fictional, from `data/sample-mockups.json`.
- FAQ: no sensors needed, languages, who it is for, pricing and trial.

## Say

"Say what happened in your own language. ARQ writes the log entry." "No sensors. Machines are logged by people."

## Do not say

Real-time monitoring, IoT, automatic downtime detection, OEE, any uptime or saving figure, any hardware or robotics claim. Any vendor name for the speech service.

## Open questions

Exact supported spoken languages (the FAQ names Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati and English; confirm). Whether "IQ" is part of the public name.
